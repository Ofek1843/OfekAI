Refund Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

We want you to be happy with FuelPhysique. If you are not, here is how our refund process works.

1. Refund requests

Before a paid launch, the owner must approve the applicable refund and consumer-cancellation terms with counsel. Until then, no fixed money-back guarantee is promised on this page. To request a review of a payment issue, email fuelphysiquesupport@gmail.com with the subject "Refund Request" and the minimum information needed to locate the transaction.

2. Renewal Charges

Subscription renewal charges are generally non-refundable. If you intended to cancel before a renewal and were charged, contact us within 48 hours and we will evaluate your request.

3. Other Circumstances

We may issue refunds in exceptional circumstances, including:

  • A billing error or technical error on our part
  • Extended service outages lasting more than 72 hours
  • Duplicate charges

Refunds will not be issued for dissatisfaction with AI-generated content alone.

4. Refund Process

If a refund is approved, it is processed through the payment provider used for the transaction, currently PayPlus when payments are enabled. Processing time depends on that provider and the payment method.

5. Contact

Refund requests: fuelphysiquesupport@gmail.com

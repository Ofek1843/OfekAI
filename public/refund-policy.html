Refund Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy does not limit mandatory cancellation, refund or other consumer rights under applicable law. Where those rights apply, they take priority over this policy.

1. Refund requests

No fixed money-back guarantee is promised on this page. To request a review of a payment issue, email fuelphysiquesupport@gmail.com with the subject "Refund Request" and the minimum information needed to locate the transaction.

2. Renewal Charges

If you intended to cancel before a renewal and were charged, contact us promptly so we can review the transaction. There is no additional 48-hour deadline imposed by this policy; any time limits and refund rights under applicable law remain unaffected.

3. Other Circumstances

We review payment issues, including billing errors, duplicate charges, service outages and problems with the service delivered. Refund eligibility is assessed under applicable law and the terms disclosed for the transaction. AI-generated content is not excluded from this review merely because it was generated by AI.

4. Refund Process

If a refund is approved, it is processed through the payment provider used for the transaction, currently PayPlus when payments are enabled. Processing time depends on that provider and the payment method.

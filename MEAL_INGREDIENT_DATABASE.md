## PHOTO GENERATION GUIDELINES

### For AI Photo Generator:

1. **Scale Accuracy**: The weight of ingredients in the photo should be visually proportional to their gram amounts
   - 100g rice looks like ¾ cup cooked
   - 150g chicken breast is roughly 5-6 oz (palm-sized piece)
   - 200g Greek yogurt fills a standard container

2. **Plate Composition**:
   - Protein (25-40% of plate)
   - Carbs (40-50% of plate)
   - Vegetables (20-30% of plate)
   - Fats (drizzled/counted, not piled)

3. **Realistic Portions**:
   - Use standard dishware (9-10 inch plates)
   - Stack/layer foods realistically
   - Account for cooked vs raw weights (rice, pasta expand when cooked)

4. **Verification Checklist**:
   - [ ] Total grams match ingredient list
   - [ ] Visual portions match listed amounts
   - [ ] Calorie totals match macros calculation
   - [ ] Photo looks appetizing and realistic

## MACRO CALCULATION FORMULA

For accuracy verification:
```
Calories = (Protein g × 4) + (Carbs g × 4) + (Fat g × 9)

Example:
45g protein = 180 cal
77g carbs = 308 cal
15g fat = 135 cal
TOTAL = 623 cal ✓
```

# Owner / counsel actions required before launch

Technical controls do not replace these owner decisions:

1. Confirm controller identity, business address, governing law, support and privacy contact details.
2. Obtain counsel review of Israeli privacy obligations, minors policy, consumer/subscription/refund terms, platform liability wording, and international transfers.
3. Execute/confirm current provider agreements and data-processing terms for Firebase/Google, OpenAI, ImageKit, Render, PayPlus, Telegram, ExerciseDB/AscendAPI (if enabled), Spoonacular (if enabled), and Frankfurter.
4. Approve a written retention schedule, backup deletion practice, legal-hold process, and report/moderation operating procedure.
5. Decide whether users aged 15–17 may use the service and how verifiable parental consent and Youth Mode will operate. Do not claim verified consent before that mechanism exists.
6. Review payment callbacks, cancellation/refund workflow, tax/invoice obligations, and PayPlus production configuration before accepting a real payment.
7. Review intellectual-property ownership/provenance of all media, food data, exercise data and generated assets.

No launch claim should state legal compliance, medical suitability, certified accessibility, or guaranteed deletion unless owner/counsel can substantiate it.

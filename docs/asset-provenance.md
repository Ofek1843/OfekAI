# Asset provenance

Repository-owned meal and exercise images must have a documented source or licence before production publication. Manual Nutrition images are catalog-slug assets under `public/images/meals`; their artwork/source record is maintained with the image-review workflow. No source URL, screenshot, customer upload, provider image, or generated image should be re-used as a product asset without a recorded right to use it.

External exercise media and Spoonacular images are disabled by default. AI-generated food images are disabled by default. Their flags are not a licence grant.

# Security incident response (operational draft)

1. Contain: disable affected route/provider credentials, preserve minimal logs, and do not expose secrets in tickets or chat.
2. Assess: identify affected systems, data categories, users, time window, and whether a reportable event may have occurred.
3. Escalate: involve owner and qualified counsel promptly; they determine notification duties and regulator/customer communications.
4. Recover: rotate exposed credentials, revoke compromised sessions/installations, deploy the reviewed fix, and validate access controls.
5. Document: record decisions, evidence references, remediation, and follow-up in a restricted incident record. Do not put health data, passwords, tokens, raw messages or private media into broad operational channels.
